# E-Commerce-Sales-Profitability-Analytics

## Project Overview

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing e-commerce sales, profitability, customer segments, products, categories, and regional performance. The project transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, KPI analysis, SQL queries, and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Project Statement

To analyze e-commerce transaction data and identify key factors affecting sales and profitability, while highlighting high-performing and underperforming products, categories, regions, and customer segments.

## Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Identify the most and least profitable products and categories.
* Evaluate regional and customer-segment performance.
* Track sales and profit trends over time.
* Calculate and monitor key business KPIs.
* Build an interactive dashboard for data-driven decision-making.

## Key KPIs

* Total Sales / Revenue
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin %
* Total Orders
* Quantity Sold
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Sales Growth %
* Profit Growth %
* Sales by Category & Sub-Category
* Profit by Product
* Regional Sales & Profit
* Customer Segment Performance

## Tools & Technologies

* **Excel** – Data cleaning and exploratory analysis
* **SQL** – Data querying, aggregation, and KPI calculation
* **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard and data visualization
* **Python** – Data preprocessing and exploratory analysis

## Business Outcome

The analysis provides a clear view of revenue and profitability drivers and helps identify opportunities for improving product performance, regional sales, customer targeting, and overall business profitability.
